At the table

Come for dinner.
Stay for the company.

At Sereva Hotel & Casino, the table is a place to settle in. Three dining moods bring together seasonal ingredients, thoughtful service and the pleasure of a meal with no hurry.

Three ways to dine

Find the table
that fits the day.

01

The Sereva Dining Room

The more considered evening choice. Imagine a seasonal menu built around a handful of expressive ingredients: something bright to begin, a generous main course, and a final note that is satisfying without being heavy. The atmosphere makes room for both an occasion and an ordinary evening made special.

02

The Terrace Table

A lighter, more spontaneous mood. Coffee and warm bread in the morning; crisp vegetables, grilled dishes and sharing plates later in the day. Shade, natural fabrics and an easy relationship with the outdoors keep the table feeling informal.

03

The Evening Lounge

For the interval after dinner, or a small meal in its own right. A concise bar menu, a well-made alcohol-free drink and a comfortable chair create a quieter alternative to the main dining room. The kitchen and drinks concepts are invitations to explore, not fixed menus or current service schedules.
