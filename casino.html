The evening collection

The night,
at your pace.

An evening at Sereva Hotel & Casino is imagined as a sequence of choices: dinner, conversation, a little music and, for adults who choose it, time at the tables.

01 / The gaming room

Atmosphere
before everything.

Soft light across green felt. Upholstered chairs. Clear space to move between the tables. A room that feels connected to the rest of the house.

The concept balances a sociable central floor with calmer seating at the edge. Guests can take a moment to observe the room, review the rules and decide whether they wish to join. There is no obligation to play and no promise of a particular outcome.

A hospitality host would help with the practical flow of a visit: where to find information, how to request a pause, and how to locate the lounge or independent support. Service is about clarity and comfort, never pressure to keep playing.

02 / Around the tables

Know the game.
Keep your perspective.

The table collection is a concept, subject to venue approval and local requirements. Confirm the actual games, rules, limits and eligibility before any visit.

01

Roulette

The wheel creates a natural centre to the room, with space to follow the sequence before taking a seat. Review the table rules and stakes first. Each spin is uncertain; previous outcomes do not make a later result more predictable.

02

Blackjack

A more focused table setting, with a clearly paced dealer-led sequence. Ask for the house rules before playing and take time to understand the decisions involved. Familiarity with a game never removes the possibility of losing money.

03

Baccarat

A quieter, formal table atmosphere for guests who prefer a concise sequence of play. Table-specific rules and limits should be explained before participation. Treat any stake as entertainment spending you may lose in full.

04

Electronic games

An alternative part of the floor, planned with comfortable seating and separation from the quieter lounge. Review the information on the machine, establish a limit and take regular breaks. No payout or return is guaranteed.

03 / The private salon

A quieter
conversation.

The private salon brings the Hotel & Casino setting into a more intimate room: walnut, marine fabrics, low conversation and attentive hospitality.

Discuss access preferences and your intended visit with the concierge. A private setting does not change the risks of gambling, and it should never be an invitation to exceed personal limits. There are no membership, minimum-spend or access claims on this website.

04 / Between the moments

A natural
place to pause.

The evening lounge is conceived as a genuine change of scene, with comfortable chairs, low music and a menu of non-alcoholic drinks alongside the bar.

Leave the tables for a conversation or end the evening there. A pause is never wasted time. The salon, dining room and hotel experience are equally open as ideas for a night that does not involve gambling.

05 / Before the evening

Arrive with a plan.

A little preparation creates room for a clearer decision. The following steps apply before any gambling visit, wherever you travel.

Confirm the essentials

Check the venue’s location, operating status, local eligibility and entry requirements directly. Ask about game rules, available limits and the process for raising a concern before making arrangements.

Set your boundaries

Choose a time to leave and a spending limit you can afford. Keep travel and everyday living funds separate. Avoid decisions made under the influence of alcohol or when feeling distressed.

Know where to turn

Ask for the venue’s self-exclusion information. If gambling is affecting your wellbeing or relationships, independent support is available for you and the people close to you.
