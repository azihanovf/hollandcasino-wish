The things you remember

Good company.
A little occasion.

Sereva Hotel & Casino is imagined as a house with different moods: a sociable salon, a private dinner and a setting where a small gathering can feel entirely personal.

01 / The piano salon

The evening has
another room.

A place for a conversation that begins over dinner and finds another hour.

The salon concept centres on the room itself: a piano, low lighting, comfortable seating and enough space to hear the person beside you. Live music is an envisioned part of its character; no artists, programme or performance dates are currently announced.

Take a seat before exploring the gaming floor, or make this the whole evening. A choice of alcohol-free drinks and small plates belongs naturally in the same setting.

02 / Your people, your occasion

Make it personal.

01

Private dinners

Begin with the table: the people, the conversation and the food they enjoy. A private-dining concept can be shaped around a milestone, a reunion or the pleasure of being together. Share your preferred group size and mood; room capacity and menu arrangements require confirmation.

02

Small celebrations

An afternoon that turns into dinner, a toast without a long programme, a room with space for familiar faces. Tell us what matters to the occasion. We will use the enquiry to understand your idea, without implying any confirmed event booking.

03

Creative gatherings

For teams or collaborators who benefit from a change of surroundings. Think about the balance between shared working time and informal conversation. Specific equipment, layouts, access and technical arrangements must be agreed before planning an event.

An evening, in three acts

Nothing needs to be rushed.

An illustrative flow for a future visit, rather than a timed event or a package available to book.

Act I

Start at the table

A seasonal meal gives the evening its centre. Choose a quiet corner or the company of the terrace, depending on your mood.

Act II

Follow your curiosity

Explore the lounge, spend time in conversation, or learn about the gaming-room concept. Participation in gambling is always optional.

Act III

Leave on your own terms

Another song, a final cup of tea or the comfort of your room. A good evening finishes when it feels right for you.

Your next chapter
