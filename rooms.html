Before you make plans

The practical details.

Can I confirm a booking on this website?

No. The form records a reservation enquiry. Dates, location, room details, prices and availability require an explicit written offer before you make a booking or travel arrangements.

How can I discuss access requirements?

Describe the practical arrangements that would help you, such as step-free access or bathroom preferences. Please avoid sending medical records. Specific room accessibility must be confirmed before booking.

Can I stay without visiting the casino?

Of course. The stay, dining and wellness concepts have their own identity. Gambling is optional and separate from the experience of being a guest.
