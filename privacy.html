How the Sereva website and its enquiry forms handle the information you choose to share.

Last updated · 18 September 2026

1. Scope and contact

This policy describes this static Sereva concept website. For questions about an enquiry or a privacy request, email hotelandcasino@welcome.com. No legal entity, operating hotel, licensed casino or registered business address is represented as verified by this website.

2. Information you submit

The reservation form sends your name, email address, preferred arrival and departure dates, adult guest count, room preference, optional notes and privacy-consent response. The contact form sends your name, email address, selected enquiry topic, message and privacy-consent response. Both forms include a submission subject, table-format instruction and an anti-spam honeypot field.

We use these details to understand and respond to your request. Do not send payment card details, passport information, identification documents, medical records or other sensitive information. This website does not process payments, create accounts or confirm reservations.

3. FormSubmit processing

Both forms use native HTTPS POST to FormSubmit, a third-party form processor, with hotelandcasino@welcome.com as the delivery recipient. Submitting the form transfers the entered information to FormSubmit and takes you to its submission flow. The recipient address must be activated by email before normal forwarding can take place. FormSubmit states that submissions received before activation are retained and forwarded after confirmation.

FormSubmit’s documentation describes a submission archive retained for 30 days. This provider archive is separate from messages forwarded to the recipient’s mailbox. We do not claim an automatic deletion period for the mailbox: contact the address above to ask about access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable obligations.

4. Spam prevention and provider cookies

We use a honeypot and retain FormSubmit’s default spam protection. Its help materials identify Google reCAPTCHA, including versions 2 and 3. The provider’s submission pages and anti-spam services may process technical information and use cookies under their own policies. The Sereva pages do not load reCAPTCHA in advance.

The FormSubmit privacy policy describes processing information to operate its service and disclosures required to provide it or comply with law. That published policy is dated 17 January 2019; it is a provider statement, not a complete independent audit of its current infrastructure.

5. Website storage and external links

The authored website does not set cookies, use browser storage, load analytics or include advertising trackers. Locally supplied styles, scripts and images support the interface. Hosting and network providers may still process ordinary connection information under their own policies.

The stay planner passes dates, guest count and room preference in the contact-page URL, where those values may appear in browser history. Names, email addresses and messages are not placed in that URL by the site. External links lead to independent services with their own privacy policies.

6. Your choices

You can browse without submitting a form. You may contact hotelandcasino@welcome.com directly by email instead; your email provider and the recipient’s email provider will then handle that message. To ask about information already submitted, identify the enquiry and request without sending unnecessary sensitive details.

Do not send an enquiry on someone else’s behalf without appropriate permission. If information is needed to handle a request, withholding it may mean the request cannot be answered.

7. Provider references

  • FormSubmit documentation and archive information
  • FormSubmit help, activation and spam prevention
  • FormSubmit privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy

Provider practices may change. Review their current documents when submitting information. Updates to this website’s behaviour should be reflected in this policy.
